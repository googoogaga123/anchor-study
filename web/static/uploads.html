Scan with your phone

Printed handouts, too.

Tap the camera button in the chat and photograph each page. A scanned PDF from your phone's own scanner app works the same way.

1

Snap each page

Take a photo per page, or choose photos you already have. Retake, reorder or remove pages before you save.

2

Straightened and read

Each photo is cropped to the page and straightened, then its text is read by OCR (text recognition). A blurry or empty photo is flagged so you can retake it.

3

Check and fix, then save

See what was read beside each photo and correct any misread word. The pages are saved as one PDF, and any answer that rests on a scanned page shows that page.

Cites the page
What happens when you add a file

Read once. Answered from every time.

1

Read

The text, tables and equations come out with their page, slide or line kept, so answers can point back to them.

2

Pictures described

Each graph, flowchart or screenshot gets a written description. Arrows are read three times and kept only where the readings agree.

3

Indexed

Your file is split into passages and indexed two ways: by its words and by its meaning, so both exact terms and plain questions find it.

4

Ready to ask

A few minutes for a typical PDF. You'll see “Added to your Library” in the chat.

Yours alone. Your Library is private: nothing you add is shared with other users. To read your files and answer your questions, their text and pictures are sent to OpenAI, the AI provider Anchor uses. Delete a file and it is removed from every search index, and its text from your saved answers.
